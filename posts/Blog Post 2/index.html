3 Background

It is no secret that football is a contact sport that can result in bodily harm. With that knowledge there is no surprise that the players in football will eventually get hurt, and with these injuries leave us as the consumers with a lot of question. From a fantasy football perspective if one of your key players get hurt then you are officially down a player for the foreseeable future and you are left fumbling around figuring out of to replace that hole you have in your team. Injuries on field can also cause in issue for the football team itself because then they could be down a star player for their whole season and will have to change their seasons plan to make up for the injury. Outside of those two perspectives you have the medical questions of how can we reduce injuries and how can we improve recovery for those players. No matter where you are at or what perspective you are looking at, injuries are a big part of football and can say a lot for the future, of sport, a career, and a season. Studying injuries is important for fantasy football because it lets you predict how long a person will be out and when they do come back will they come back at their fullest potential. Also, Studying these injuries is an important part of the sport and is something that needs to be done for improvement of said sport. In fact the study of injuries have already been used to improve the longevity of the players that play this sport.” then-league commissioner Paul Tagliabue took a big, if seemingly begrudging, step. Despite describing concussions as merely “an occupational risk,” he created a brain injury committee. Since then, dozens of new rules, new equipment, and stiffer penalties have been put in place, all with the intention of trying to reduce the number of concussions players sustain.” (Most,2024) The NFL has add multitude of new rules in the hopes of reducing brain injuries toward their player. So, again, studying injuries is important to help the sport live.

3.1 The Research

In this study I am looking to find out what position gets hurt the most? Hypothesize that the more contact heavy positions are the positions that will get hurt the most out of any other position. When I say contact heavy I am looking at TE and RB because TE are on the line and go out for routes so they are most likely to make contact more and RB have to run thought the line which means they to have a high chance to make contact. I also believe that these positions are the positions that get hurt the most because of the continual contact leads for more wear and tear and ultimately in injury, they might not get hit the hardest but I bet more consistent contact will out weigh how much impact the hit has.I predict that out of all the injuries in the NFL RBs will be injured the most.

4 Method

To test my question I will get a list of all the injuries that have happened since 2009. With that list I will look at the specific positions and how many times that position got hurt, To see once in for all what position gets injured the most.
